**Mat Launcher 1.7.0**

Folders and a "what's new" tour after every update.

### New
- **Folders.** In edit mode, drop an app onto the middle of another app to make a folder, or onto an existing folder to add to it. Tap a folder to open it, rename it, or take apps back out. Folders can be dragged and rearranged like apps. Dropping on the left or right edge of an app still just reorders.
- **What's new screen after every update.** The first time you open a new version, a short tour shows the new features, with switches to turn them on or off right there (and a shortcut to accessibility settings where needed). Skip it any time.

### Also in 1.6.0
- **Settings in categories.** Settings opens to a list of pages: Updates, Wallpaper, Icons, Home & widgets, Lock screen, Control center, Apps, Permissions, Setup & about.
- **Drag apps to rearrange** in edit mode, including between pages (hold at the screen edge).
- **Automatic GitHub releases** for every new version.

### Notes
- Android still shows an install confirmation for every update.
