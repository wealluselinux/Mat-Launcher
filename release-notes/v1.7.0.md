**Mat Launcher 1.7.0**

New Material You icons and a redesigned Mat Weather.

### New
- **New Mat Launcher icon.** Dark background, an outlined, rounded home with an arched door inside a thin ring, and Material 3 "cookie" shapes in the top-left and bottom-right corners. It also has a themed (monochrome) version for Android 13+.
- **New Mat Weather icon** in the same style: a cookie-shaped sun behind a cloud on a dark background, with a themed version.
- **Mat Weather redesign.**
  - A big hero card with a cookie-shaped condition icon, slowly turning cookie shapes in the background, and high / low pills.
  - An **hourly forecast** for the next 24 hours, with rain chances.
  - A 2×2 details grid: feels like, humidity, wind, rain chance.
  - A 7-day forecast with temperature range bars.
  - A soft color tint at the top that follows your wallpaper colors.

### Notes
- Android still shows an install confirmation for every update.
- Your launcher may keep showing the old icon for a moment after updating; restart the launcher or wait a few seconds.
