**Mat Launcher 1.6.0**

Folders, drag-to-rearrange, categorized Settings, a "what's new" tour after updates, and automatic releases.

### New
- **Folders.** In edit mode, drop an app onto the middle of another app to make a folder, or onto an existing folder to add to it. Tap a folder to open it, rename it, or take apps back out. Folders can be dragged and rearranged like apps. Dropping on the left or right edge of an app just reorders.
- **Drag apps to rearrange.** In edit mode, long-press an app and drag it to a new spot, including onto another page (hold it at the screen edge).
- **Settings in categories.** Settings opens to a list: Updates, Wallpaper, Icons, Home & widgets, Lock screen, Control center, Apps, Permissions, Setup & about. Tap one to open its own page.
- **What's new screen after every update.** The first time you open a new version, a short tour shows the new features, with switches to turn them on or off right there. Skip it any time.
- **Automatic GitHub releases** for every new version number.

### Improved
- While editing, taps no longer launch apps and the swipe gestures are paused so they don't get in the way.

### Notes
- Android still shows an install confirmation for every update.
