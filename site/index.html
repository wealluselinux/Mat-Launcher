What you get

Wallpaper-matched icons

Themed, transparent or glass icons in circle, squircle, rounded or teardrop shapes. Or pick your own image per app.

Control center

Swipe down for flashlight, volume, brightness, rotation, Do Not Disturb and quick links to connectivity.

Lock screen

A Material You clock lock screen with optional PIN. Double-tap the home screen to lock.

Widgets

Clock (digital or analog), weather and a photo slideshow, ready on first launch.

Mat AI

A built-in on-device helper: open apps, check weather, do math, restyle your icons. Rule-based, no cloud.

Mat Weather

A bundled Material You weather app, offered on first run. Powered by Open-Meteo.

Install

1. Download MatLauncher.apk from the latest release and install it.
2. Press Home and pick Mat Launcher as your home app.
3. Say yes to installing Mat Weather when asked.
